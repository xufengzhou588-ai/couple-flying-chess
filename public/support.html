Support

Need help with room invites, QR scanning, online sync, purchases, or game content? Send a support request below. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the room-code flow you were trying to use if the issue is related to online play.

Common checks

  • For online rooms, both partners should use the same app version and join the same room code.
  • For QR scan, allow camera permission and keep the QR code fully visible on the other device.
  • If sound does not play, make sure Silent Mode and system volume are not blocking app audio.

Privacy questions are handled under the Privacy Policy.
