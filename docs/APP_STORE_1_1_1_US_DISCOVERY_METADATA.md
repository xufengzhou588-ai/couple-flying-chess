```text
Make date night feel like a game for two. Couple Flight brings a flirty board game and four quick ways to play, laugh, and get a little closer.

CHOOSE YOUR GAME

Flight Chess — Take turns around a playful board and see which challenge comes your way. Play together on one phone, or invite your partner with a room code when you are apart.

The Slow Burn — Privately pick a spark you both feel good about, then play out a short scene together.

Chemistry Dice — One die picks an action. The other picks a body part chosen by the partner receiving the move. Roll, tease, reroll, or pass.

Sketch Our Date — Draw an idea on the screen, let your partner guess, then choose a date you would actually try.

Date Night Mystery — See different clues, tell each other what you found, and solve the night together.

The four mini games are made for two people sharing one phone. Flight Chess also offers optional two-device play by room code.

Start where your relationship is: a first date, a familiar Friday night, or a long-overdue evening together. Keep the moment comfortable for both of you. You can always change or skip a prompt.

Available in English, Spanish, and Chinese.
```
