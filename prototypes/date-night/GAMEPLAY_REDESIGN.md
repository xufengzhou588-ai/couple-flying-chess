# Mini-game lineup: make each mode play differently

Decision, 2026-11-07: the eight-mode preview repeats a read/respond/reveal loop too often. Keep two text-led modes while replacing the rest of the public mini-game shelf with games that use the phone and the room differently. Unlisted modes remain in source for reference, but are not part of the current player-facing lineup.

| Mode | Player action | Why a couple would replay it | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Slow Burn | Privately agree on a comfortable flirtatious move, then play a short scene | Consent-aware chemistry and a different shared scene | Keep as the one dialogue-led romance mode |
| Date Night Mystery | Share private clues and solve a three-stop date together | Cooperative deduction with a different answer each stage | Keep as the one clue-led mode |
| Chemistry Dice | Privately choose comfortable body parts, then roll one die for an action and one for a shared spot | Spontaneous, physical play with an easy pass and no surprise boundary crossing | Rebuild |
| Sketch Our Date | Draw a date idea on the phone; the other partner guesses and picks one to try | Creative reveal and a concrete next-date idea | Build |

Secret Mission, Double Yes, First Date Again, Read My Mind, and Wish Auction are hidden from the public shelf until they have distinct interactions. Do not repackage the same questions under new names. This is a four-mode mini-game preview alongside the existing Flight Chess board; expand only after testing whether couples actually replay these four.

The US App Store listings for [Candle](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/candle-couples-relationship/id6743355635) and [Foreplay](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/foreplay-couples-date-night/id6739634971) show the useful pattern: questions are one activity among photos, synchronous touch, drawing, wheel/dice, and board play. This is a reference for interaction variety, not evidence that any one mechanic will sell in this app.

Translation follows gameplay freeze. Player-facing English, Simplified Chinese, and US Spanish copy should be reviewed against `LOCALIZATION_QA.md` after the modes stop changing.
